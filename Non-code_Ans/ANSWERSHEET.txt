Part 1:
1: Structs have member access be public as default because 
a carry-over from C. Classes have member access be private as
default, instead having the accessors be public.

2: TObject is the default class for any class in ROOT. Inhereting
from it means we get prepackaged error handling, sorting, printing,
drawing etc.. It guarantees that our class functions and can be 
stored within ROOT.

3: Compile using ".L MovingObject.cxx+"

Part 2:

4 and 5: Load and use speedcheck.C after compiling MovingObject, 
it checks that the setter and getters work properly. The result 
should be that for example A the total speed is 0.5 while for B 
it is 0.707107.

The reason why B has a higher total speed without changing the
horisontal speed is that when raising vz the object has to move 
an extra distance in the same time as before, meaning higher speed, 
but this distance is orthogonal to the horisontal plane spanned 
by vx and vy meaning the total speed in that plane is unchanged.

6: We can verify the amount of entries by looking at the tree->Print()
output. The seed used was 55555 and the parameters were a mean of 
0.0 and deviation of +-1.5 for all three axes.

Part 3:

7: In Velocity_components.png you can see a saved scatterplot and
histogram from the implementation in Part 3. 

In the scatterplot we that the horisontal speed can go up to 
almost 7 in very rare cases but that most entries gather between 
-1 to 1 of Z and around 1.5 to 2.5 of the horisontal speed.

In the histogram we see that most entries gather around origo 
but that there are some buckets in the quadrant where both x and y
are positive that contain up to 14 entries.

The gathering of the entries in both plots is a result of our 
generation parameters (mean 0 with an error of +-1.5) while the 
specific gathering in the positive quadrant of the histogram is 
likely complletely random, seeing as there's nothing in our code 
directing entry-generation within our parameters.

Before running, make sure you load write.C and read.C.
